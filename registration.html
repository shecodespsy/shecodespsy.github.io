APPLICATION GUIDELINES

In the application form you will be kindly asked to upload a motivation letter (1-page extension) written in English.
In this letter, we would like you to tell us:

  • Why you are interested in participating in this course
  • How you think learning basic programming skills could be useful for your academic and/or professional development.

We are genuinely interested in understanding your personal motivation for applying.
Communicating why you want to join is more important to us than writing the letter in perfect English.
For this reason, we strongly encourage you not to use artificial intelligence tools (ChatGPT or similar) to write your motivation letter.
We want to hear your own voice, experiences, and expectations.
