DESCRIPTION

La Corrala de Santiago is a historic building located in the Realejo district of Granada, one of the city’s most traditional neighborhoods. Dating from the 16th and 17th centuries, it is a representative example of popular communal housing organized around a central courtyard. After being restored, the building was transferred in 1991 to the University of Granada and now serves as a residence for visiting scholars and guests. Integrated into the cultural life of the Realejo, La Corrala de Santiago also functions as a venue for academic and cultural activities associated with the University.
