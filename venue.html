How to get Cartuja Campus

By bus

Several bus lines connect Granada city center to Cartuja Campus (U1, U2, U3, 8).

If you are staying at Corrala de Santiago, we recommend taking Line 8:

  • Where to take it: Gran Vía 14 – Catedral (direction Cno. de Alfacar)
  • Where to get off: Farmacia 2

The CIMCYC building will be right in front of you.

We recommend checking updated routes and timetables on Granada’s urban bus website or using Google Maps for real-time directions.

By car

  • Free parking available at various points on campus.

On foot

  • The campus can be reached on foot, but be prepared for a long and steep uphill walk.

How to reach Granada from Málaga Airport

By bus (recommended)

  • Right outside the arrivals exit at Málaga–Costa del Sol Airport, you will find long-distance buses.
  • The main company operating this route is ALSA.
    You can check schedules and book tickets on their website by selecting:
    From: Málaga Airport
    To: Granada Bus Station
    We strongly recommend booking your ticket in advance, as buses can sell out quickly.

    When choosing your departure time, allow at least 1–1.5 hours after your flight arrival, to account for delays and airport procedures.
