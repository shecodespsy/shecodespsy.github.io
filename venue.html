How to get Cartuja Campus

By bus

  • Several bus lines connect to Cartuja Campus from September to July:
    Line U1 (JPG), Line U2 (JPG), Line U3 (JPG)
  • Check updated information on Granada's urban buses

By car

  • Free parking available at various points on campus

By plane

  • Federico Garcia Lorca Granada-Jaén Airport is located in Santa Fe, about 20 minutes away by bus or taxi (fare around €20).
