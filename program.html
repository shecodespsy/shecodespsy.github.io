Information for participants

REQUIREMENTS

To ensure that you follow the workshop activities smoothly, we need to make sure that you have access to all the relevant materials and services, which include:

  • access to Eduroam, using the credentials provided by your home university
  • your own laptop or tablet
  • a Google account

MATERIALS

The course will be delivered using Google Colab.
Course materials will be provided there, and participants will use Colab for hands-on exercises, coding practice, and scripting during the sessions.
The access link will be shared at the beginning of the course.

MEALS

Coffee breaks and lunch will be provided during the 3 days of the workshop (13, 14, 15 of May) at the CIMCYC.
Dinner expenses are not covered and will be at your own cost.

KEEP IN TOUCH!

We have set up two Telegram spaces to communicate with.
The link has been sent to the participants via email.

CERTIFICATE OF ATTENDANCE

A certificate of attendance will be issued to participants at the end of the course.
Attendance is mandatory, and the certificate will be granted only to participants who attend at least 80% of the course activities.
