Aims and scopes

Join us for a 3-day immersive course designed for psychology students to learn basic programming skills while actively addressing gender stereotypes in STEM.
Through hands-on, collaborative projects and guided mentoring, you will gain confidence in coding and explore its relevance for psychological research. The course fosters an inclusive and supportive learning environment, especially for students with little or no prior programming experience.

Event funded by the Arqus Alliance and the University of Granada (Mind, Brain and Behavior Research Center - María de Maeztu Excellence Unit, and the Master in Behavioral and Cognitive Neuroscience).

Topics

  • Python basics (variables, data types, control flow)
  • Computational thinking (problem decomposition, patterns, algorithms)
  • Functions & libraries (working with tools for research)
  • Data handling & simple analysis (intro to real Psychology-style data)
  • Project-based teamwork (collaborative coding in international groups)
