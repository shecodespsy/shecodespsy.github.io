Social Events

Granada is a fascinating city that blends centuries of history with a vibrant university atmosphere.
We wish your experience here goes beyond programming, and get to know the unique culture and lifestyle of this city.
That's why we've organized a series of social activities throughout the workshop, to help you connect with each other and experience Granada.

SOCIAL EVENTS

Wednesday, May 13th: TAPAS NIGHT

  • Experience Granada's legendary tapas culture.
    We'll enjoy an evening tasting some of the city's traditional dishes – a perfect opportunity to relax, socialize, and discover the flavors of Andalusian cuisine.
    Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options will be available.

Thursday, May 14th: GUIDED TOUR OF ALBAICÍN

  • We'll explore the Albaicín, one of Granada's oldest and most enigmatic neighborhoods.
    This UNESCO World Heritage Site will take you on a journey back in time through its maze-like streets, leading you to stunning viewpoints overlooking the city.
